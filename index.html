I study how Markets are made.

Streetwear in high fashion, craft coffee, the creator economy: each became a market because people built it. My research shows who built them and how, and what firms need to do to shape a market.

Pierre-Yann Dolbec · Associate Professor of Marketing, John Molson School of Business, Concordia University
