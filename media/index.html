Bio

Short bio

Pierre-Yann Dolbec is Associate Professor of Marketing at the John Molson School of Business, Concordia University, in Montréal. He studies how markets are made and change, from streetwear and coffee to creators and platforms. His research appears in the Journal of Consumer Research, Journal of Marketing Research and Journal of Marketing.

Long bio

Pierre-Yann Dolbec is Associate Professor of Marketing at the John Molson School of Business, Concordia University, in Montréal. He studies how markets are made and change, and how companies and consumers deal with the complexity this creates. His work covers markets for fashion and streetwear, coffee, sneakers, music, education and platform services such as Uber and Airbnb, as well as the creator economy. It appears in the Journal of Consumer Research, Journal of Marketing Research, Journal of Marketing and Academy of Management Journal, and has been presented at the World Economic Forum. He has received close to $1M in research funding and sits on the editorial review boards of six journals, including the Journal of Consumer Research and the Journal of Consumer Psychology. His work has been covered by CBC, Radio-Canada, CTV News, Global TV, Channel News Asia, Le Devoir, Les Affaires and the National Post. He gives interviews in English and French.
